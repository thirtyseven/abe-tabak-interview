A NOTE FOR READERS

A transcript is a starting point.

Automatic transcription and speaker detection help locate a passage; they do not settle every word, name, or memory. Uncertain readings remain visible, and family corrections are added over time.

“Published” describes access to a working reader. “Family reviewed” means a relative has confirmed a complete listening pass. Those are separate milestones.
